Privacy notice

For the Hero Made Smart waitlist, run by Hero Made Labs (Hawaii). "We" means Hero Made Labs. Last updated 2 November 2026.

What we collect

Only what you type into the waitlist form: your email, what draws you to the app, the test you're prepping for (if you tell us), what you'd pay, your phone type, and that you're 18 or older. We also note which link brought you here (for example, a video). Our waitlist doesn't record your IP address, and this page uses no ad trackers.

How we use it

To email you about Hero Made Smart's launch, and to invite a few people to a short, optional chat about the app. Your answers also help us decide whether and how to build it.

Who sees it

Only the Hero Made Labs team. The list is kept in a private Google Sheet. Google stores the sheet for us, and GitHub, which hosts this page, keeps standard server logs. We never sell your answers or share them for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until the app launches, or until we decide not to build it. After that, we delete it unless you've become a user.

Your choices

Email smart@heromadelabs.com any time to see, change or delete your information, or to leave the list.

Age

The waitlist is for people 18 and older.
